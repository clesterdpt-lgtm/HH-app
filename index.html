Field-based reality

Home health is mobile, inconsistent, and messy. CareNote is being built for clinicians working in the field, not behind a front desk.

More than AI notes

The goal is not to tack AI onto charting. The goal is to support the actual workflow of a home health visit — from capture to organization to export.

🎙️

Voice capture + transcription

Record your observations naturally and convert them into usable text for note generation, clarification, and documentation cleanup.

🤖

AI-generated clinical notes

Generate structured notes for evaluations, routine visits, reassessments, recerts, discharges, and more from real-world visit details.

🧠

Assist before finalizing

Use AI to identify missing details, tighten your raw notes, and improve documentation quality before the final note is exported.

💊

Medication manager

Track medications, save medication history, and support reconciliation-style workflows in a way that fits home visit documentation.

🏃

Home exercise program builder

Create and manage HEPs with structured exercise selection, patient-friendly output, and documentation support for the clinical record.

📆

Unified history + calendar

See note activity, medication history, and HEP changes together by date so your workflow is easier to track over time.

Designed for the field, not just the desk

Home health care happens across driveways, kitchens, living rooms, and spotty cell service. CareNote is being shaped around that reality.

Mobile-first workflow Capture and organize information while moving between visits instead of rebuilding the day from memory later.
Offline-aware behavior Queue work locally and keep going when connectivity is inconsistent.
Practical exports Notes, PDFs, email outputs, and documentation support designed for how clinicians actually need to share or transfer information.
Less after-hours charting The entire point is to reduce friction, reduce rework, and reduce the amount of unfinished documentation following you home.

Who CareNote is for

If your work happens in patients' homes and your documentation load follows you from visit to visit, this is the kind of workflow CareNote is meant to support.

Physical therapists

Support evaluations, reassessments, HEP creation, gait and balance documentation, and ongoing visit notes.

Occupational therapists

Document function-focused interventions, patient education, home-based training, and daily living support more efficiently.

Speech-language pathologists

Capture observations clearly, organize visit details, and support consistent documentation across episodes of care.

Home health nurses & clinicians

Support structured note creation, medication workflows, and longitudinal care tracking across the home setting.
